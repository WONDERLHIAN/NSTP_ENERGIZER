Rules of the Game: Red Light, Green Light

  1. 1. Everyone must start in a standing position.
  2. 2. A random color, either RED LIGHT or GREEN LIGHT, will appear on the screen.
  3. 3. When RED LIGHT appears, everyone must sit down immediately.
  4. 4. When GREEN LIGHT appears, everyone must remain standing.
  5. 5. The last person to follow the instruction or anyone who makes the wrong move will be chosen to answer a question.
  6. 6. After the question is answered, the game continues with another random color.

Remember to pay attention, react quickly, and follow the instructions carefully. Good luck, everyone!
